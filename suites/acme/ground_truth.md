# Acme Home Goods — customer policies

Owner-provided ground truth used by the monitor to grade answers.

- Acme Home Goods is an online store selling kitchenware, bedding and home decor.
- Items can be returned for a full refund within 30 days of delivery.
- Returned items must be unused and in their original packaging. Sale items are final sale and cannot be returned.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the return.
- We ship to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom only.
- Standard shipping is free on orders over $50; otherwise it costs $5.99.
- Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.99.
- Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm Eastern Time.
- Support email: help@acmehome.example. Support phone: 1-800-555-0142.
- Acme Plus is a membership costing $9 per month that includes free express shipping.
- To cancel Acme Plus, go to Account > Membership > Cancel membership. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month.
- Customers can request deletion of their account data at Account > Privacy > Delete my data. Deletion completes within 10 business days.
- Acme does not offer price matching.
- Small appliances carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty.
- Gift cards never expire and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Acme gift cards.
- Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of being placed.
- Acme Rewards gives 1 point per dollar spent; 100 points are worth $5 off.
